Rage House Donations

Do you have junk just laying around the house or in the garage? If so, consider donating to Rage House. We accept all breakables,  glass and ceramics –  plates, bottles, cups, glasses,  vases & coffee mugs; kitchen appliances including toasters, blenders, coffee makers, microwaves & crock pots. We  also accept broken or damaged electronics that you do not have a use for  any more – flat screen TV’s, alarm clocks, computers, cameras, office phones, laptops,  monitors, stereos, keyboards and game consoles.  We also accept wooden furniture –  nightstands, bookshelves, wooden chairs, & tables. 

WE DO NOT ACCEPT ANY ITEM THAT MAY BE CONSIDERED HAZARDOUS OR TOXIC

(If unsure, give us a call or send a text with an attached photo)

 WE CAN PICK UP or YOU CAN DELIVER TO US 

(either way please use the button below and we will contact you)
